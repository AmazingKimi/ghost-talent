# Responsible Use Boundary

Ghost Talent is a public-evidence research intelligence tool. It is **not** an automated employment-decision system and should not be used as the sole or determinative basis for hiring, rejection, compensation, promotion, or other employment decisions.

## Current boundary

Ghost Talent currently:

- analyzes public technical evidence,
- produces research-oriented discovery and recommendation signals,
- exposes evidence and uncertainty,
- excludes sensitive personal attributes from ranking,
- requires human technical review before any real-world decision.

Ghost Talent does not claim legal compliance for every jurisdiction or recruiting workflow. Organizations adopting it are responsible for their own legal, privacy, employment, and data-governance review.

## Prohibited product assumptions

Do not treat:

- `STRONG SIGNAL` as “hire”,
- `EARLY SIGNAL` as “contact automatically”,
- `WATCH` as a negative employment judgment,
- low public visibility as evidence of low ability,
- missing public evidence as evidence of poor performance.

## Data minimization

The ranking model should remain focused on public technical evidence necessary for the research question. Sensitive traits and unrelated personal information are outside scope.

## Human review

Candidate Dossiers are designed to support a technical reviewer asking whether the public evidence is worth inspecting further. They are not substitutes for code review, interviews, references, consent-sensitive recruiting processes, or organizational hiring policy.

## Future enterprise work

Before ATS/CRM integrations, automated outreach, or production employment workflows are added, the project should complete a dedicated legal/privacy design review and define jurisdiction-appropriate controls, retention policy, correction mechanisms, and auditability requirements.
